# Warranty Policy

## Standard Warranty
All electronics (headphones, keyboards, speakers, and similar items) carry a
**1-year manufacturer warranty** against defects in materials and
workmanship, starting from the delivery date.

## What's Covered
- Manufacturing defects
- Premature battery failure (electronics)
- Component failure under normal use

## What's Not Covered
- Damage from drops, water, or misuse
- Normal wear and tear (e.g., fraying cables after extended use)
- Damage from unauthorized repairs or modifications

## Filing a Warranty Claim
Contact support with the order number and a description of the issue.
Support may request photos or a short video of the defect. Approved claims
are resolved via repair, replacement, or refund, at our discretion.

## Extended Warranty
An optional 2-year extended warranty can be purchased at checkout for
eligible electronics, extending coverage to 3 years total from delivery.

## Non-Electronics Items
Apparel, drinkware, and home-goods items are covered under the standard
30-day return policy rather than a separate warranty — see the Returns
Policy for defective-item handling beyond 30 days.
