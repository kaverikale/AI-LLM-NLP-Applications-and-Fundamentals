# Returns & Refunds Policy

## Return Window
Items may be returned within **30 days** of the delivery date for a full
refund, provided they are unused and in original packaging.

## Non-Returnable Items
- Final sale / clearance items
- Personal care items (once opened)
- Gift cards

## How to Start a Return
1. Go to "My Orders" and select the item.
2. Choose a return reason and print the prepaid return label.
3. Drop the package off at any carrier location within 14 days of starting
   the return.

## Refund Timing
Refunds are issued to the original payment method within 5–7 business days
of the returned item being received at our warehouse. Store credit refunds
are issued within 1 business day instead.

## Damaged or Defective Items
Items that arrive damaged or defective can be returned free of charge,
outside the normal 30-day window, for up to 90 days from delivery. Customers
should provide a photo of the damage when starting the return.

## Exchanges
We do not process direct exchanges. Return the original item for a refund
and place a new order for the replacement.

## Order Cancellations
Orders can be cancelled free of charge only while status is "processing."
Once an order has shipped, it must go through the standard return process.
