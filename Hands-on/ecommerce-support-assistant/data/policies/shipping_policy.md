# Shipping Policy

## Processing Time
Orders are processed within 1–2 business days. Orders placed after 2 PM ET
are processed the next business day.

## Shipping Options
- **Standard (5–7 business days):** Free on orders over $50; otherwise $5.99.
- **Expedited (2–3 business days):** $12.99.
- **Overnight (1 business day):** $24.99. Order must be placed before 12 PM ET.

## Order Tracking
A tracking number is emailed once an order ships. Tracking can also be
looked up in "My Orders" on the account page.

## International Shipping
We currently ship to the US, Canada, and the UK. International orders may
be subject to customs fees, which are the customer's responsibility.

## Shipping Delays
Delays caused by the carrier (weather, logistics disruptions) are outside
our control. If a package is delayed more than 3 business days past its
estimated delivery date, contact support for a resolution — this may
include a partial refund of shipping costs or reshipment.

## Lost Packages
A package is considered lost if tracking shows no movement for 10 business
days. Report a suspected lost package to support; we will file a claim with
the carrier and reship or refund once confirmed.
